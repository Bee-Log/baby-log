Privacy policy

Baby Log. Last updated 9 October 2026.

This page says what Baby Log does with information.

The short version

  • Baby Log is a client web app. It is a web page whose code runs in your own browser, on your own phone.
  • Baby Log has no server and no database. It has no accounts of its own.
  • The Baby Log project does not receive, store or keep any of your information. No data is persisted by Baby Log.
  • Your information is kept in places you own: your phone's browser storage and, only if you sign in, a hidden folder in your own private Google Drive.

Where your information is kept

You type in the information. It can include:

  • a baby's nickname, date of birth, sex and an optional photo;
  • feeds, sleeps, nappies, crying, weight and length, and any notes you add.

Baby Log shows and edits this information. It does not hold it. The information is kept in two places:

  • Your phone. Your browser keeps a copy in its storage (IndexedDB), so the app works with no internet. It stays on that phone until you delete it.
  • Your Google Drive. Only if you sign in. It is saved in your own private Google account, in a hidden folder that only Baby Log can open. The Baby Log project cannot see it.

What Baby Log asks Google for

Signing in with Google is optional. It is only used to keep two phones in step.

  • The app asks for one permission: drive.appdata. This lets the app see, create and delete only its own hidden folder in your Google Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • The app saves your entries as text files in that hidden folder. Each phone writes its own file. The other phone reads them.
  • The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or any other Google information.
  • Google gives the app a sign-in token that lasts about one hour. The app keeps it in your phone's browser storage until it ends or you tap Sign out on this phone.

Baby Log's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your information

  • Nothing is sent to the people who made the app, because the app has no server to send it to.
  • The only network calls the app makes are to Google: to load Google's sign-in script, to sign in, and to read and write the hidden Drive folder.
  • The app does not sell, share or give your information to anyone. It does not use it for adverts. It has no analytics or tracking.
  • The app's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub can see standard visit details, such as an IP address, when a page loads. See GitHub's privacy statement. Your entries are not sent to GitHub.

Cookies

Baby Log does not use cookies. It uses browser storage on your phone for your entries and for the sign-in token. Google's own sign-in window may use Google's cookies. That is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • On the phone: delete an entry in the app, or clear the site's data in your browser settings. This removes everything saved on that phone.
  • In Google Drive: open Google Drive, then Settings, then Manage apps. Find Baby Log and choose to delete its hidden app data. Google may change these menus.
  • Stop the app's access: visit your Google account permissions and remove Baby Log. You can also tap Sign out on this phone in the app.
  • Take a copy: in the app, open Sync and data to download all entries as CSV or JSONL.

Children

Baby Log is made for parents. It is not for use by children. The information about a baby is entered by the parents, and it stays under their control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.

Questions

Ask a question or report a problem on the project's GitHub issues page. Please do not post your baby's information there.
