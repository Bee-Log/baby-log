Baby Log

A newborn routine log for two parents. Last updated 9 October 2026.

Baby Log is a small web app for two parents. They use it on their phones to write down feeds, sleep, nappies, crying, weight and length. They often do this at night, with one hand.

What it does

  • It is a client web app. Its code runs in your browser, on your phone. It has no server and no database.
  • It keeps entries in your phone's browser storage, so it works with no internet.
  • It can keep the two parents' phones in step. For this, a parent signs in with a Google account. The entries are then saved in a hidden folder in that parent's own private Google Drive.
  • The Baby Log project does not receive, store or keep any of your data.
  • It shows weight and length against the World Health Organization (WHO) child growth standards.
  • It can export all entries as a spreadsheet (CSV) or a text file (JSONL).

Who runs it

Baby Log is a private family project. It is not a company and it has no paid plans, no adverts and no tracking.
